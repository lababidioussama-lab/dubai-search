FAQ

Questions

How do I save to Photos?

Tap Save to Photos, then choose Save Video (or Save Image) in the menu that opens. If the button says “Tap again to save”, tap it once more. Very large videos open instead — tap Share → Save Video there.

How do I get the MP3?

Choose “MP3 audio” before tapping Download. The audio is converted to MP3 right on your device, then saved to Downloads (Files app on iPhone). Very long audio (over ~20 MB) can't be converted.

Can I save photos?

Yes — TikTok photo slideshows and photo posts on most sites. Use “Save to Photos” or “Save all”, then choose Save Images.

Why isn't my video in the quality I picked?

You get the best version the site offers up to that quality. Some sites (like YouTube) only offer low quality as a single file with sound.

A link doesn't work

Private posts, stories that expired, and age-restricted content can't be fetched. Instagram and YouTube sometimes limit downloads from servers — try again a little later.

Is this legal?

Saving videos for personal use is generally fine. Only re-upload or share content you own or have permission to use.
